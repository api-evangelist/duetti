# Duetti

> Duetti is a music finance and catalog services company that buys music rights — masters, publishing shares, and royalty percentages — directly from independent artists, songwriters, and creators for upfront cash, with no debt, interest, or recoupment. Founded in 2022 by Lior Tibon and Christopher Nolte and headquartered in New York, it has raised more than $435 million and works with 1,100+ music creators.

**No public API.** Duetti publishes no developer portal, no OpenAPI or other machine-readable
API contract, no SDK, and no MCP server. Its artist-facing applications (DUO, statements
vault, sync portal) are authenticated web apps, not documented API surfaces. This file was
generated by API Evangelist from the company's public web presence, not served by Duetti.

## What Duetti does

- [What We Do](https://www.duetti.co/what-we-do): catalog purchases (masters, publishing, royalty shares), distribution, royalty tracking and claiming, marketing, and sync
- [Artist Partners](https://www.duetti.co/artist-partners): eligibility and the artist-facing offer — tracks at least 6 months old earning $2,000+/year
- [Remix Program](https://www.duetti.co/remix-program): commissioned remixes of Duetti-managed tracks with upfront cash and no distribution fees
- [About Us](https://www.duetti.co/about-us): company background, leadership, and newsroom
- [Request a Meeting](https://www.duetti.co/request-a-meeting): the intake path for artists and rightsholders
